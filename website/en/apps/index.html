Get Onyx

Apps for everything you do.

They’re made for Onyx, look alike and work together. Most are already on the card; the rest install in one click.

All Work The web and your people Create Watch and listen Play Learn and tinker The system

Work

A full office suite that reads the files people send you.

Letters

The word processor. Letters, reports, properly laid‑out documents.

  • Opens and saves Word (.docx), OpenDocument (.odt) and RTF files
  • Tables, headers and footers, a table of contents, pictures
  • Mail merge and PDF export

Spreadsheet

The spreadsheet. Your budgets, your lists, your charts.

  • 237 functions, written the way Excel writes them
  • Charts, conditional formatting, filters
  • Opens and saves Excel (.xlsx) and CSV files

Slides

Presentations. Polished slides, shown full screen.

  • Six themes, shapes, tables, charts
  • Transitions, animations and a presenter view
  • Opens and saves PowerPoint (.pptx) files; PDF export

Calendar

The planner. Your appointments by day, week or month.

  • Repeats, reminders and task lists
  • Several calendars, each in its colour
  • Imports and exports Google and Outlook calendars (.ics)

Ledger

Accounting for Belgian businesses and the self-employed.

  • Quotes, orders, invoices and credit notes, printed through Letters
  • Bank statements imported, supplier payments prepared for your bank
  • VAT returns, balance sheet and income statement

Cardfile

A small database, no jargon. Your contacts, collections, inventories.

  • Design your card, then fill it in
  • Card view or list view, sorting and search
  • CSV import and export; feeds the mail merge in Letters

PDF Viewer

The PDF reader. One tab per document, and it remembers your page.

  • Thumbnails, contents and text search
  • One page, continuous or two pages side by side; full screen
  • Copy the text, follow the links

Clock

The time here and around the world, alarms, a timer and a stopwatch.

Notes

Quick notes that save themselves. Pin the ones that matter to the desktop.

Text Editor

For plain text, simply.

Calculator

A scientific calculator, by keyboard or mouse.

Graphing Calculator

Plot up to four functions, live as you type them.

Archiver

Open, create and change ZIP archives by dragging files in and out.

RTF Reader

A quick look at an RTF document.

They work together.

Send a photo by mail straight from Photos. Merge a Cardfile address list into a letter. A link in a PDF opens in Jet. What you copy here pastes there, and the clipboard keeps your last ten copies.

The web and your people

Browse, write, chat.

Jet Browser

The Onyx web browser, built on WebKit, the engine behind Safari.

  • Shows today’s websites, video and sound included
  • One page per window, to keep your focus
  • Find in page, zoom, downloads

Mail

All your mail accounts in one inbox.

  • Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo, or the mail account you already have
  • Messages grouped into conversations; search across every account
  • Contacts, attachments, a notification when mail arrives

Telegram

Your Telegram account, in a messenger with early-2000s charm.

  • One window per conversation
  • Hand-drawn emoticons, photos sent and received
  • Notifications and online status

Unofficial client; Telegram is a trademark of its owner.

IRC

IRC chat rooms, presented like a modern messenger.

Create

Pictures, music, objects.

Paint

Drawing and painting on layers.

  • Brushes, airbrush, crayon, marker, calligraphy
  • Layers and blend modes, gradients, text, selections
  • Opens PNG, JPEG, GIF and BMP; colour adjustments and filters

Koton

The music studio. Write a song starting from its chords.

  • A chord track that drives the accompaniment, the melody and the drums
  • Instruments and effects as plugins; a USB MIDI keyboard just works
  • WAV export; a full manual in English and French

3DForge

Design parts for 3D printing, step by step.

  • Sketch a shape, give it depth, join, cut, round the edges
  • Every step stays editable: change a measure and the part rebuilds
  • Preparation for 3D printing and for machining

Screenshot

Capture an area, a window or the whole screen, then mark it up.

Icon Editor

Draw icons, pixel by pixel.

FM Tracker

Music with the sound of vintage computers, on eight voices.

Mandelbrot

Dive into fractals, one click at a time.

Watch and listen

Your photos, your music, your films.

Photos

All your photos in one place, sorted by day.

  • Albums, favourites and search by date or camera
  • Crop, straighten, adjust the light, apply a filter
  • Slideshow, send by mail, set as wallpaper in one click

Media Player

Your music and your videos, in one library.

  • Music by artist, album or genre; your own playlists
  • Films and series, resumed where you stopped
  • Plays MP3, FLAC, OGG, MP4, MKV, WebM and more

Image Viewer

Open a picture with a double click, zoom, move on to the next.

Play

Sixteen games, and a library for the consoles you grew up with.

Pinball

A pinball table with real physics.

  • Three tables: a space station, a haunted manor, a volcano
  • Keyboard or gamepad; a top five for each table
  • In English and French

Critters

Lead little creatures to the exit by giving some of them a job.

  • Climber, builder, digger and more
  • Twelve levels, from training to expedition
  • In English and French

Solitaire

Classic patience, with cards you drag.

FreeCell

Every card face up, 32,000 numbered deals.

Doom

The classic shooter, shipped with the free Freedoom levels.

Arkanoid

A brick breaker with power-ups, written in BASIC on Onyx.

Invaders

Hold off the fleet before it reaches the ground.

Pipes

Lay the pipes before the water comes.

Tetris

Stack the pieces, clear the lines.

2048

Merge the tiles up to 2048.

Minesweeper

Clear the field without touching a mine.

Sokoban

Push every crate onto its spot.

Snake

Eat, grow, don’t bite yourself.

SameGame

Clear groups of the same colour.

Pong

Two players on one keyboard, first to nine.

Game of Life

Draw some cells and watch them evolve.

Game Library

All your console games on one shelf.

  • A tile per game, sorted by console
  • Starts the right emulator; works from a gamepad
  • Watches the folders you choose

Emulators

  • Game Boy / Game Boy Color
  • Game Boy Advance
  • NES
  • Super Nintendo

The Game Library sorts the games you own and starts the right emulator: Game Boy and Game Boy Color, Game Boy Advance, NES and Super Nintendo. Saves are kept beside each game, and a USB gamepad is all you need. No game ships with Onyx.

Console names are trademarks of their respective owners; Onyx is not affiliated with any manufacturer.

Learn and tinker

To see how things work, or to make your own.

Turtle Quest

Learn to program by guiding a turtle.

  • 48 levels, from first steps to fractals
  • Step by step, with a hint and a lesson along the way
  • In English or French

Circuits

A puzzle game about how a computer computes.

  • 20 levels, from a single wire to an adder
  • The circuit comes alive as you build
  • In English and French

QBStudio

Make your own windowed apps, in BASIC.

  • Draw the window with the mouse
  • Write only what each button does
  • One click and it joins your apps

QBasic

Old-school BASIC, with its blue editor and examples to run.

GPIO Lab

The Raspberry Pi’s 40 pins on screen: try an LED or a button, even with nothing plugged in.

Courier

For web developers: send requests and inspect the answers.

The system

The tools that keep the house in order.

File Viewer

Your folders in columns, with a preview.

  • Drag and drop, copy, cut, paste
  • A Trash you can restore from
  • USB sticks and favourite folders in the sidebar

Control Panel

Theme, display, sound, keyboard, Wi-Fi, printers: every setting.

Package Manager

Install, update and remove apps in one click.

Disks

Your cards and USB sticks: free space, eject, format.

Task Manager

What’s running, the memory in use, and a way to stop an app that’s stuck.

Terminal

The command line, for those who like it.

And more keep coming.

The Package Manager offers new apps as soon as they are published.
