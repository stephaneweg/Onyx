Doom for Onyx
=============
Doom (doomgeneric) looks here for its IWAD, in this order: doom2.wad, plutonia.wad, tnt.wad,
doom.wad, doom1.wad, freedoom2.wad, freedoom1.wad. Onyx ships Freedoom Phase 1
(freedoom1.wad: free content, BSD licence, see FREEDOOM-COPYING.txt). Copy your own Doom
files here to play the original game (the shareware doom1.wad works too).
Opening a .wad in the File Viewer starts Doom with it. Settings: default.cfg; saved games:
savegame/<iwad>/.
