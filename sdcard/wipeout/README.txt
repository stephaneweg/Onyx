wipEout for Onyx -- the game's files
====================================

Onyx has the game's program (wipeout-rewrite, by Dominic Szablewski), not its files: the
textures, the 3D models, the sounds and the tracks are Sony's and are not part of Onyx.
Bring them from your own copy of the game, into this folder (SD:/wipeout), as wipeout-rewrite
expects them (its README: https://github.com/phoboslab/wipeout-rewrite):

  SD:/wipeout/textures/            the PlayStation (NTSC) version's data,
  SD:/wipeout/track01/ ...         with a few menu models of the PC version
  SD:/wipeout/common/
  SD:/wipeout/sound/
  SD:/wipeout/music/track01.qoa    the music, in QOA format (optional)
  SD:/wipeout/intro.mpeg           the intro film, MPEG-1 (optional)

The game writes its settings and best times here too: SD:/wipeout/save.dat.

Keys: the arrows steer, X thrust, Z fire, C / V the air brakes, A the view, Enter pause,
Esc / Backspace back; F11 or Alt+Enter full screen. A gamepad: the d-pad or the left stick,
A thrust, X fire, L / R the air brakes, Y the view, Start pause.
